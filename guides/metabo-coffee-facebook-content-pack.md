## 5. Engagement mechanics (make the algorithm work for you)

Facebook's feed rewards *comments and shares* far more than likes. Every post should earn one.

- **Keyword comment CTA** — "Comment BALANCE and I'll send the details." Converts passive readers into a DM list you own.
- **Two-option poll** — "Team ☕ Morning Coffee or Team 🌙 Afternoon Slump? Comment your side."
- **Fill in the blank** — "My 3PM energy right now is ____ out of 10."
- **Tag-a-friend, framed as care, not spam** — "Tag the friend who reheats the same cup four times a day."
- **Reply to every comment within the first hour.** Early comment velocity is the single biggest ranking signal you control for free.
- **Share the flagship post to your Story** with a "Swipe up / link in bio" sticker the same day it goes live.

## 8. Compliance guardrails (protects your ad account)

Meta rejects health and weight-management ads aggressively. These rules keep you running:

**Never:**
- Promise specific results — "lose 10kg," "drop 2 sizes," "guaranteed"
- Use before/after body photos, or any close-crop of a body part
- Imply the reader is unattractive, overweight, or should feel bad — Meta's "personal attributes" policy bans copy like *"Are you fat?"* or *"Tired of your belly?"*
- Claim it treats, cures, or prevents any disease or condition
- Show a scale, BMI chart, or measuring tape

**Always:**
- Use *supports*, *helps*, *aids*, *promotes* — the same hedged verbs already printed on your packaging
- Keep testimonials real and unedited; get written permission before posting a name
- Include the disclaimer below on any post making a benefit claim
- Say "supports your weight management journey," never "makes you lose weight"

**Standard disclaimer (paste at the end of benefit posts):**

```
This product is a dietary supplement and is not intended
to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease. Results
vary from person to person. Best paired with a balanced
diet and regular exercise.
```

## 10. Quick wins beyond the feed

- **Pin the flagship carousel** to the top of the Page.
- **Reels:** film one 15-second clip of the sachet being torn, poured, stirred, and lifted — no face needed. Caption it with hook #1. Reels get roughly 3–5× the organic reach of static images right now.
- **Boost the testimonial post only.** It's the one with proof and a CTA, so it converts coldest traffic best. Start at a small daily budget for 3 days and read the cost per comment before scaling.
- **Save every commenter who types your keyword** into a DM follow-up list. Organic reach fades; a DM list doesn't.
