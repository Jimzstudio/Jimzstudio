# 10-Second Script — Blockchain Investment

**Format:** vertical 9:16 short (TikTok / Reels / Shorts)
**Runtime:** 10 seconds
**Tone:** calm, anti-hype, "the grown-up in the room"
**Angle:** blockchain as infrastructure, not a lottery ticket

## Clean voiceover (~34 words, ~3.5 words/sec)

> Blockchain isn't a lottery ticket. It's infrastructure.
> So invest like it — small amounts, on a schedule, only what you can afford to lose.
> Two or three assets you actually understand. Cold wallet.
> Boring wins.

---

## Production notes

- **Pace:** every line lands on a cut. No shot longer than ~1.2s except the final freeze.
- **Captions:** burned-in, large, high contrast — most views are muted.
- **Framing:** keep text out of the bottom 20% (platform UI covers it) except the disclaimer.
- **Do not** name specific coins, quote returns, or imply guaranteed gains — that's what gets short-form finance content suppressed or reported. The script deliberately stays at principle level.
- **Compliance:** keep the "Not financial advice" overlay in the export, not just the caption.
