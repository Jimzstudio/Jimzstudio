# Nutrasa — "The Root of Health Begins at the Cellular Level"

**Format:** Brand / corporate video
**Runtime:** 3:00
**Narration:** ~400 words (documentary pace, ~135 wpm, with room to breathe)
**Tone:** Calm, scientific, warm. Authority without hype.
**Voice:** Single male or female VO, mid-range, unhurried. Music: soft cinematic strings + subtle pulse (heartbeat motif) building from 1:35.

## PRODUCTION NOTES

- **Timing:** Read cold at ~135 wpm this lands at 2:52–2:58, leaving 2–8 s of tail for the logo sting. If the VO runs long, trim Sequence 5's food-science sentence first — it's the most compressible line.
- **Pacing:** Hold silence for a full beat after "…protect and restore itself" (1:12) and after "what are we feeding our cells?" (0:42). Those two pauses carry the film.
- **Product roll-call (2:12–2:38):** ~0.9 s per product. Cut on the name, not before it. If the list feels rushed, drop to eight hero products on screen and let the remaining four appear as a grid.
- **Music:** No drums until 1:36 (Nutrasa reveal). Pull music down 6 dB under the Blobel/NASA section so the science reads as fact, not advertising.
- **Subtitles:** Burn in EN; prepare BM and 中文 subtitle tracks — product names stay in English across all versions.
- **Cutdowns from this master:** 60 s = Seq 1 + 3 + 5 + 7. 30 s = Seq 1 + 5 + 7. 15 s social = Hook option 2 + product wall + logo.

## CLAIMS & COMPLIANCE CHECKLIST

Read before locking VO — these choices are deliberate:

1. **"May" is doing real work.** Energy, metabolism, recovery, and cellular ageing lines all use *may contribute / may affect*. Keep that wording; stating them as certainties turns a marketing claim into a medical one.
2. **"100% bioavailable" and "clinically proven" were left out of the VO** and replaced with "predigested nutrition, in a form the human cell is ready to absorb." Absolute absorption claims and unqualified clinical claims typically need study data on file. If you hold the substantiation, this line can be inserted at 2:12: *"Every formulation is backed by scientific testing — and results you can measure."*
3. **Blobel and NASA are context, not endorsement.** The script deliberately never says either endorses, tested, or is connected to Nutrasa. Keep it that way in the edit — don't place the Nutrasa logo in the same frame as the Nobel medal or a NASA insignia, and use licensed or generic-equivalent footage for the space sequence.
4. **Organ-named products** (Nephrocare, Livercare, Cardiowell, Ocucare, Immunotherapy) are named on screen only — the VO makes no treatment claim about any of them. Do not add "for kidney disease / liver repair" style supers; in most markets that reclassifies the product as a drug.
5. **Business-opportunity line** is kept to "a simple, achievable opportunity" — no income, earnings, or lifestyle promise. Add a visible disclaimer super if this cut is used in recruitment settings.
6. Add a standard footer super on the final frame if required in your market, e.g. *"This product is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease."*
