# Video Script — "The Root of Health Begins at the Cellular Level"

**Format:** Brand / product film
**Duration:** 2 min 30 sec (150 seconds)
**VO word count:** ~345 words (target pace ≈ 140 wpm — unhurried, warm, authoritative)
**Tone:** Calm, scientific, human. Not a hard sell. Documentary-brand feel.
**Voice:** Mature male or female, mid-low register, measured pace, no hype inflection.

> Replace `[BRAND]` and `[PRODUCT]` with the actual names before recording.

## Full Script

### SCENE 1 — THE HOOK (0:00 – 0:12)

**VISUAL:** Black screen. A single slow heartbeat of light. Cut to extreme macro — skin, blood flow, then a slow push into a glowing cell.
**ON-SCREEN TEXT:** *(none yet — let the visual breathe)*
**SFX/MUSIC:** Deep low hum, single sustained note. Soft heartbeat.

**VO:**
> Everything you call "health" — your energy, your metabolism, how quickly you recover — none of it begins in the mirror. It begins somewhere you have never seen.

---

### SCENE 2 — THE CELLULAR TRUTH (0:12 – 0:28)

**VISUAL:** Wide shot of a person in morning light, then dissolve into a cellular field — trillions of points of light forming the shape of a human body.
**ON-SCREEN TEXT:** `THE ROOT OF HEALTH BEGINS AT THE CELLULAR LEVEL`
**MUSIC:** Strings enter, slow build.

**VO:**
> Inside you are trillions of cells. They are the smallest working unit of your body — and the root of your health begins right there. At the cellular level.

---

### SCENE 3 — WHY IT MATTERS (0:28 – 0:48)

**VISUAL:** Split-screen contrast. Left: a bright, active, well-nourished cell. Right: a dim, sluggish one. Cut to real life — someone struggling up stairs, staring at a laptop at 3pm, rubbing their shoulder after exercise.
**ON-SCREEN TEXT:** `HEALTHY CELLS → HEALTHIER BODY`
**MUSIC:** Rhythm tightens.

**VO:**
> Healthy cells build a healthier body. But when cells are not properly nourished, it can show up in the way you feel every single day — lower energy, a slower metabolism, a longer road to recovery.

---

### SCENE 4 — THE LONG-TERM COST (0:48 – 1:03)

**VISUAL:** Time-lapse — a calendar, seasons changing, the same person year after year. Macro cells slowly losing their glow.
**ON-SCREEN TEXT:** `LONG-TERM DEFICIENCY MAY AFFECT CELLULAR AGEING`
**MUSIC:** Drops to near silence on "no longer works the way it used to."

**VO:**
> And over time, long-term nutritional deficiency may contribute to cellular ageing — and to a body that no longer works the way it used to.

### SCENE 5 — THE SHIFT IN THINKING (1:03 – 1:22)

## Production Notes

- **Music:** One continuous track, three movements — mystery (0:00–1:03), turn (1:03–1:42), triumph (1:42–2:34), resolve (2:34+). Do not change tracks mid-film.
- **Colour:** Cold blue-grey in Scenes 1–4, warming to gold from Scene 5 onward. The colour shift carries the narrative.
- **Product placement:** Product does not appear until Scene 10. This is a science story first, a product film second.
- **Subtitles:** Burn in for social. On-screen text is written to work with sound off.
- **Cutdowns from this master:** 60s (Scenes 1, 2, 3, 6, 7, 10) and 30s (Scenes 1, 2, 6, 10) can be pulled from the same VO recording — record the full read in one session.

## Compliance Notes (read before locking the script)

1. **Hedged language is deliberate.** "May affect," "may contribute to," "can show up as" — keep these intact. Do not let anyone upgrade them to "will," "cures," "restores," "reverses," or "prevents." Most markets (including Malaysia's KKM/NPRA and comparable regulators) treat unqualified claims on supplements as drug claims.
2. **No disease references.** The script never names a condition, and it should stay that way.
3. **Verify before broadcast:** the "40+ years," "space food," and international-collaboration references need to be backed by documentation you actually hold. I wrote these from the brief you gave me — I have not verified them independently. Same for Dr. Lim's title and credentials; confirm the honorific spelling and any titles shown on screen.
4. **Testimonials:** none used here. If you add any, they need their own disclaimer per market.
