# 15-Second Script — Forex Investment

**Format:** vertical 9:16 short (TikTok / Reels / Shorts)
**Runtime:** 15 seconds
**Tone:** calm, anti-hype, "read the fine print" authority
**Angle:** forex isn't the danger — leverage is. Survival before returns.

## Clean voiceover (~50 words, ~3.4 words/sec)

> Your forex broker is legally required to publish how many of their clients lose money.
> Go read that number.
> Forex isn't the scam. Leverage is. Fifty-to-one turns a two percent move against you into a wipeout.
> So risk one percent a trade. Journal every single one. Trade one pair until it's boring.
> Survive first. Then compound.

## Production notes

- **The 50:1 math is real, use it as-is:** at 50:1 your margin is 2% of the position, so a 2% adverse move erases it. That's why the graphic should drain to exactly zero, not "mostly down."
- **Localize the leverage number.** 50:1 matches US retail major pairs (CFTC/NFA cap). For an EU/UK audience swap the VO and graphic to 30:1 and a 3.3% move — the ESMA cap. Don't ship one number to every market; a wrong cap reads as amateur to the exact audience you want.
- **Pace:** no music for the first 3.5s. The silence is the hook — everything in this niche opens on a beat drop, so not doing that is the pattern interrupt.
- **Captions:** burned-in, large, high contrast — most views are muted.
- **Framing:** keep text out of the bottom 20% (platform UI) except the disclaimer.
- **Do not** show a live P&L screenshot, name a broker, quote a win rate, or imply a return. Those are the fastest routes to suppression, and on Meta they can trip financial-services ad policy even on organic posts.
- **Compliance:** keep both disclaimer lines burned into the export, not just the caption.
