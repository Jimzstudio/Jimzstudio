# Forex — 15-Second Short-Form Video Script

For TikTok / Reels / YouTube Shorts / Xiaohongshu video. Vertical 9:16, 15 seconds, educational (not a signals or "get rich" pitch — see the compliance note at the bottom).

**Target VO length:** ~38–42 words. At a natural short-form pace (~2.7 words/sec) that lands at 14–15 seconds without sounding rushed.

---

## Version A — "What forex actually is" (default)

| Time | Voiceover | On-screen text | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:02 | "Every time you swap money on holiday, you've traded forex." | **YOU'VE ALREADY TRADED FOREX** | Hard cut on a hand sliding cash across an exchange counter. No intro, no logo. |
| 0:02–0:06 | "Forex is the market where one currency is priced against another — like EUR/USD." | `EUR / USD` | Two currency symbols snap into frame, split screen. Ticker line animates between them. |
| 0:06–0:10 | "If EUR/USD is 1.10, one euro costs one dollar ten. That number moves every second." | **1.10** → **1.1043** (digits ticking) | Big number center frame, last two digits flickering. Subtle candle chart behind. |
| 0:10–0:13 | "Traders try to profit from those moves. Most lose money doing it." | **~3 IN 4 RETAIL ACCOUNTS LOSE MONEY** | Chart snaps red. Number lands hard, no motion. |
| 0:13–0:15 | "Learn the mechanics before you risk a cent." | **FOLLOW FOR PART 2: PIPS & LEVERAGE** | Cut to talent looking at camera, or clean text card. |

**Caption:** The world's biggest market, explained in 15 seconds. Part 2 covers pips and leverage. Educational only — not financial advice.

## Production notes

- **Hook is the whole video.** The first ~1.5 seconds decide retention. Open on a concrete image (cash, a ticking number), never on a logo or "hey guys."
- **Pace:** cut every 2–3 seconds. Five to six shots total.
- **Read the VO ~10% faster than feels natural,** then trim silence in post rather than cutting words — 15s runs out faster than it reads.
- **On-screen text is not the VO.** Keep it to 3–5 words per card so it's readable at thumb-scroll speed; caption the VO separately with auto-subtitles.
- **Audio:** low-BPM trending sound at ~15% under the VO. Drop the music out at 0:13 so the closing line lands dry.
- **Safe zones:** keep text out of the top 12% and bottom 20% (UI overlays) — roughly 250px top / 400px bottom on a 1080×1920 frame.
- **Numbers must be current.** Check the EUR/USD level the week you shoot; a stale rate reads as a stale account.

## Compliance note

Financial content gets restricted or removed on most platforms when it promises returns. Keep to these:

- Include "educational only / not financial advice" in the caption, and on screen if your market requires it.
- Never show or imply profit figures, account balances, or win rates.
- The "~3 in 4 retail accounts lose money" figure comes from broker risk disclosures (the exact percentage varies by broker and is usually stated in the 70–80% range). Cite the specific figure from a current disclosure, or soften to "most retail traders lose money."
- If you're promoting a broker, affiliate link, or paid course, disclose it — platform rules and advertising regulators both require it.
