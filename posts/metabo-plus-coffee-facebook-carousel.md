**Format:** 10-slide carousel (1080 x 1080 px)
**Objective:** Stop the scroll → build curiosity → drive swipes → click to order
**Placeholders to fill before publishing:** `[PRICE]`, `[SITE/WHATSAPP]`, `[KEY INGREDIENTS]`, `[OFFER]`

## 2. Slide-by-slide

### SLIDE 1 — The hook (this slide does 80% of the work)

**Headline (huge, top 2/3 of frame):**
> ## I changed ONE cup.
> ## Not my diet. Not my gym.

**Sub-line (small, bottom):**
> Here's what happened in 21 days 👉

**Visual:** Extreme close-up of coffee being poured, steam catching the light, warm morning kitchen tones. Text on a solid dark overlay so it's readable at thumbnail size. **No product packaging on slide 1** — a visible product = an ad = a scroll-past. Keep the brand mark tiny in the corner.

**Design rule:** the headline must be legible on a phone at 1/4 size. If you have to squint, the font is too small.

## 5. Posting notes

- **Best slots:** 6:30–8:00am (the "I'm making coffee right now" window) or 8–10pm. Check Metricool's best-time data for your actual audience before locking it in.
- **Reply to every comment in the first 60 minutes** — early comment velocity decides whether Facebook pushes the post further.
- **Reply with a question**, not just "Thanks!" — "How many cups are you on before you feel awake? 😅" turns one comment into a thread.
- **Repurpose:** slides 1, 4 and 7 each work as a standalone Reel or Story with a "swipe up for the full story" sticker.
- **If you boost it:** carousels are cheap to test — run hooks A vs D at a small daily budget for 3 days, then put everything behind the winner.

## 6. Compliance check before you publish

Facebook rejects health/wellness ads for these specific things, so keep them out:

- ❌ **No before/after photos** or images implying body transformation — an automatic rejection.
- ❌ **No "lose X kg in Y days"** or any guaranteed-result claim.
- ❌ **No negative self-perception** ("Tired of being overweight?", close-ups of body parts being "fixed").
- ❌ **No disease/treatment claims** ("cures", "treats", "fixes your metabolism").
- ✅ **Do** talk about energy, focus, routine, taste, convenience — that's where this copy lives, and it converts better anyway.
- ✅ Add your market's required disclaimer if the product is a supplement, e.g. *"Results vary. Not intended to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease."*

The copy above is deliberately written around **energy, focus and ritual** rather than weight loss — that keeps it publishable and keeps the promise honest.
