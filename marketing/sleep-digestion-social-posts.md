# Social Post Pack — "Easy to Sleep, Easy Digestion"

Campaign angle: one product, two everyday benefits people already feel the lack of —
falling asleep without a fight, and a stomach that behaves after meals.

**Before publishing:** replace every `[PRODUCT]`, `[BRAND]`, `[LINK]`, and `[PRICE/OFFER]`
placeholder. Health claims below are written as comfort/lifestyle language, not medical
claims — keep them that way unless you hold approved claim substantiation for your market.

---

## Image notes (applies to whichever photo you upload)

| Placement | Ratio | Crop guidance |
|---|---|---|
| Facebook feed | 1200 x 630 (landscape) or 1080 x 1080 | Product left or right third, keep clear space for overlay text |
| Instagram feed | 1080 x 1350 (4:5 — takes the most screen space) | Product centred, breathing room top and bottom |
| Instagram Stories / Reels cover | 1080 x 1920 | Keep the product in the middle 60% — top/bottom get covered by UI |

**Overlay text options (pick one, keep it to ≤6 words):**
- `Sleep easy. Digest easy.`
- `Rest well. Feel light.`
- `Wind down. Settle in.`
- `Easy nights, easy mornings.`

Keep the overlay in one weight of one typeface, high contrast against the photo, and leave
the bottom 20% of the image clear so the caption/CTA doesn't fight it.

### Hashtag notes
- Keep it to 10–15 relevant tags. Mix broad (#GutHealth) with narrow (#BedtimeRoutine) —
  broad tags alone bury you.
- Rotate the set between posts; identical hashtag blocks post after post get down-weighted.
- Put them after a couple of `.` line breaks, or in the first comment.

### Story frames (3-frame sequence)

1. **Hook** — the photo, overlay: `Still awake at 1am?` + a poll sticker: *Yes / Every night*
2. **Cause** — plain background, text: `A heavy stomach doesn't let you sleep.`
3. **Product + CTA** — the photo again, overlay: `Sleep easy. Digest easy.` + link sticker
   to [LINK]

### Reel idea (10–15s, if you want to extend the shot)
Hook (0–2s): tossing and turning, text on screen `Every. Single. Night.`
Turn (3–7s): the evening routine — pouring/taking [PRODUCT], text `One small change after dinner.`
Payoff (8–12s): calm morning shot, text `Sleep easy. Digest easy.`
End card: [BRAND] logo + `Link in bio`.
Use a soft, slow trending audio — not an upbeat one; the mood is the message.
