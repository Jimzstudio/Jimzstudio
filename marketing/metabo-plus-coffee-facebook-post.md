# METABO+ Coffee — Facebook Post Content Pack

Brand: **PRINSES Aesthetics Beauty**
Product: **METABO+ Coffee** — instant white coffee, 15 sachets (20g), *supports metabolism & healthy lifestyle*
Assets: 4 square (1:1) creatives — hero pack shot, 3-step how-to, "regular coffee crash" graph, comparison graph

> **Claim rule for every version below:** we sell the *feeling of a better morning*, not weight loss.
> Never write "burn fat", "lose X kg", "slimming", "detox", or before/after promises — that language gets
> ads rejected under Meta's health & personal-attributes policy and creates legal exposure for a supplement.
> Safe territory: taste, ritual, convenience, steady energy, "supports metabolism", how it makes mornings feel.

---

## 1. Recommended posting order (4-image carousel)

Facebook carousels are swiped, so the order *is* the story. Use this sequence:

| Slide | Image | Job it does |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Your day on regular coffee" (crash graph) | **Hook** — names the pain before naming the brand |
| 2 | "The problem was never the coffee" (comparison graph) | **Curiosity** — reframes the villain, teases the fix |
| 3 | "Real white coffee. Just built smarter." (pack shot) | **Reveal** — the product finally appears |
| 4 | "Your new 30-second morning" (tear/pour/stir) | **Reduce friction** — it's this easy, right now |

If you post a **single image** instead, lead with the crash graph — it is the strongest scroll-stopper because it looks like information, not an ad.

## 2. MAIN POST (use this one first)

**Primary text:**

> By 2PM you're not tired. You're *crashing*.
>
> And you'll blame the late night, the meetings, the weather — anything except the cup you drank at 8AM.
>
> Here's the part nobody tells you: that 2PM wall isn't a sign you need more coffee. It's the receipt for the coffee you already had. Spike hard at 8, freefall by 2, reach for cup #3, repeat tomorrow.
>
> So we asked a very annoying question: what if the problem was never the coffee… but what was *missing* from it?
>
> That question became METABO+ Coffee. ☕
>
> Real white coffee — smooth, creamy, no bitter edge — built to support your metabolism and a healthy lifestyle instead of just spiking you and leaving. One sachet. Hot water. Stir. 30 seconds, and your morning is done.
>
> 15 sachets. 15 mornings. Swipe to see what the difference actually looks like 👉
>
> 💬 Comment **"MORNING"** and we'll send you the details.
>
> #METABOPlus #PrinsesAestheticsBeauty #WhiteCoffee #MorningRitual #HealthyLifestyle #CoffeeLovers #Metabolism #SelfCare

**Why it works:** opens on a specific moment the reader has lived (2PM), accuses the obvious suspect and clears it, then withholds the answer for one more beat ("what was missing from it") before the reveal. The comment prompt is one word, so it's easy to obey — and comments push reach far harder than link clicks.

## 7. Meta Ads notes (if you boost this)

- **Do not** target or imply weight, body shape, or health conditions in copy or targeting — that's a policy rejection *and* a personal-attributes violation ("Struggling with your weight?" ❌).
- Speak to the *product*, not the *person*: "steady mornings" ✅ vs. "your slow metabolism" ❌.
- The crash-graph creative has the lowest text-density and the strongest thumb-stop — use it as the primary ad image, with the pack shot as the second variation.
- Add a disclaimer line where required by local rules, e.g.
  *"Dietary supplement. Not intended to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease. Best paired with a balanced diet and regular activity."*
- Best posting windows for this audience: weekday **7:00–9:00 AM** (they're literally making coffee) and **8:00–10:00 PM**.
