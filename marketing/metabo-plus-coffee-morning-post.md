## Notes before posting

- Keep hashtags in the first comment on Instagram if you prefer a cleaner caption — reach is the same.
- Facebook down-ranks posts with links in the caption body; if reach matters more than clicks, move the shop link to the first comment.
- **Claims discipline:** stick to the language on the pack — "supports metabolism & healthy lifestyle." Avoid weight-loss promises, "burns fat," "fast results," before/after framing, or anything implying it treats a condition. Those trigger ad rejections on Meta and create regulatory risk.
- If this runs as a paid ad, Meta's text-heavy-image rules and supplement ad policies apply — keep on-image text minimal (the pack label is fine) and target 18+.
- Add the standard disclaimer where required by your market: *This product is a dietary supplement and is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease.*
